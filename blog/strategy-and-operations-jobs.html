Strategy and operations jobs: the career switch role most people never search for

Strategy and operations roles accept 0 to 2 years far more often than PM roles, pay $105k to $139k at entry level, and lead into product, finance and leadership. What the job is and how to get one.

If you're interested in business, data and product but keep hitting walls applying for product manager roles, there's a job family you may be overlooking: strategy and operations, often called BizOps, S&O or business operations. These teams solve a company's most important business problems with data and structured thinking. They hire from consulting, banking, analytics, and increasingly from new grads and career changers. And they're far more open to early career people than product management.

The numbers

In early November 2026, Crossway's feed held 319 strategy and operations roles in the US posted in the previous 45 days, with 186 posted in the last week. Of the 213 that state years:

What strategy and operations teams actually do

The work varies by company, but it usually includes:

  • Owning a business metric: marketplace health, supply in a region, unit economics for a product line.
  • Diagnosing problems: why did margins fall in one city, why are deliveries late on Fridays, why did a customer segment churn?
  • Running experiments and launches: testing a pricing change, launching a new market, piloting a new process.
  • Building models: forecasts, capacity plans, business cases for new investments.
  • Working across teams: product, finance, sales and operations all depend on S&O to connect the numbers to decisions.

Think of it as part consultant, part analyst, part product manager, inside one company.

Who's hiring

DoorDash leads by a wide margin with 51 open S&O roles, including 22 entry level ones with titles like "Associate, Strategy & Operations" and "Associate Manager, Merchant Strategy & Operations". SpaceX (24), Anduril (21), Oracle, Amazon and JPMorgan Chase follow, and Roblox, Brex and Verkada are hiring business operations associates too. Marketplaces, logistics, fintech and fast growing startups all rely heavily on these teams.

Skills they ask for

The most requested skills in our feed: Excel, SQL, go to market strategy, Tableau, Python, pricing, roadmapping and Salesforce. In practice, three skills carry most interviews:

  • Spreadsheet modeling: build a clean model with clear inputs, and change assumptions quickly.
  • SQL: pull and join data yourself. Many S&O teams expect it at the associate level.
  • Structured problem solving: break a vague question into parts, estimate, and recommend.

What the interviews look like

S&O interviews borrow from consulting. Expect business cases ("profit fell 15% in our largest market, why?"), estimation, a spreadsheet or SQL exercise, often a take home model, and behavioral questions about influencing without authority. Our business operations case questions walk through break even, capacity, pricing and market entry, and case interview practice covers the format.

Who makes a good S&O candidate

  • Consultants and bankers are the classic hires.
  • Analysts who want more ownership of decisions.
  • Operators from logistics, retail or restaurants who understand the frontline and learn the data.
  • MBA students and grads, for associate manager roles.
  • New grads with strong quantitative skills and evidence of solving messy problems.

Where S&O leads

Strategy and operations is one of the best launch points in a company. Common next moves: product manager (you already work with data, launches and tradeoffs), general manager of a region or business line, finance and strategy leadership, or chief of staff. Many PMs at marketplace and fintech companies started in S&O.

How to get your first S&O role

  1. Get sharp at Excel and SQL. Practice building a unit economics model and pulling data with joins and window functions.
  2. Practice cases out loud, timed, two or three a week.
  3. Write one business memo: pick a company, find a problem in its public numbers, propose a fix with rough math. Link it in applications.
  4. Target the right titles: Associate, Strategy & Operations; Business Operations Associate; BizOps Analyst; Strategy Analyst; Associate Manager, Operations.
  5. Apply early and find one person on the team to reach out to.

A typical week in S&O

  • Monday: review the weekly business metrics for your area and flag anything unusual.
  • Tuesday: dig into why late deliveries spiked in two cities, pulling data in SQL and talking to the local ops leads.
  • Wednesday: update a pricing test model and write a short recommendation for the product team.
  • Thursday: present findings to a director with a clear ask: pause the change in one city, scale it in three.
  • Friday: plan next quarter's targets with finance.

The common thread: turning messy data and real world operations into clear decisions.

A worked S&O case

Prompt: "Gross margin in our largest city fell from 18% to 14% in a quarter. What happened and what would you do?"

Break it down: margin is revenue minus costs over revenue. Split revenue into orders and average order value; split costs into labor, delivery, refunds and discounts.

Suppose you find: order volume is up 10%, average order value is flat, delivery cost per order rose 20%, and refunds doubled. Digging further, a new suburb was added, with longer delivery distances and more late orders, which drive refunds.

Recommendation: add a delivery fee or minimum order for the new suburb, adjust courier zones to shorten trips, and set a target for late order rate. Estimate the margin recovery from each lever, and propose a two week test.

The spreadsheet skills that matter most

  • Clean structure: inputs, calculations and outputs on separate tabs.
  • Lookups and pivot tables to join and summarize data fast.
  • Scenario toggles so you can show best, base and worst cases.
  • Simple charts that make one point each.
  • Labeling every assumption so someone else can audit it.

A one page business memo template

A short memo is the best proof you can bring to an S&O application. Pick a company with public numbers, such as a marketplace or retailer, and write:

  1. The question: one sentence, like "Should this company raise delivery fees in suburban markets?"
  2. The answer: your recommendation in two sentences.
  3. The numbers: a small model with clear assumptions, such as order volume, fee change, expected drop in orders and margin impact.
  4. The risks: what could make you wrong.
  5. The test: how you'd check before rolling it out everywhere.

Link it at the top of your application. It does more than any line on a resume to show you can do the job.

Questions to expect

  • "Revenue is up but profit is down. Walk me through how you'd figure out why."
  • "How many couriers do we need in a new city for the first month?"
  • "We want to launch a subscription. How would you price it?"
  • "Tell me about a time you changed someone's mind with data."
  • "Here's a dataset. In 30 minutes, tell me the one thing leadership should know."

Typical growth path and pay

A common path runs from associate to senior associate to manager, then to senior manager or director of strategy and operations, usually over five to eight years. Entry level postings in our feed list a typical $105k to $139k, and many S&O roles at tech companies add equity. Because the work touches every part of the business, S&O people often move into general management, product or finance leadership as they grow.

Questions

What is a strategy and operations job?

A role on a team that solves a company's key business problems with data: owning metrics, diagnosing problems, running experiments and building models.

Do strategy and operations jobs hire entry level?

Yes. In our November 2026 count, 28% of S&O postings that state years accepted 0 to 2, and 138 of 319 US roles were entry level.

How much do strategy and operations associates make?

Entry level S&O postings that list pay typically range from $105k to $139k a year in the US.

What skills do I need for strategy and operations?

Spreadsheet modeling, SQL, structured problem solving and clear communication. Tableau and Python help.

Can strategy and operations lead to product management?

Yes. It's a common route, especially at marketplace and fintech companies, because the work already involves data, launches and tradeoffs.
