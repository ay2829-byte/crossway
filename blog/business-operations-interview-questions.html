Business operations and strategy case questions, solved

Break even, capacity, pricing and market entry: the case questions ops and strategy interviews ask, with worked answers and a simple way to structure them.

Ops and strategy interviews are about structured thinking with numbers. The math is rarely hard. What separates good answers is a clear structure, a sanity check, and a decision at the end.

A simple structure for any case

  • Clarify the goal and what a good outcome looks like.
  • Break it down into a few drivers you can estimate.
  • Calculate out loud, rounding where it doesn't change the answer.
  • Decide and name the biggest risk.

Break even

A bakery has $9,000 in monthly fixed costs. A loaf sells for $6 and costs $2.50 to make. How many loaves to break even? Contribution per loaf is $3.50. 9,000 ÷ 3.50 = 2,571.4, so 2,572 loaves a month.

Capacity and staffing

A phone line gets 90 calls an hour, each taking 6 minutes. How many agents? That's 9 hours of work arriving every hour. To keep agents about 75% busy so the queue doesn't explode, you need 9 ÷ 0.75 = 12 agents.

Pricing

A product sells 10,000 units at $50 with a $30 cost. Price elasticity is −1.5. Should you raise the price 10%? Volume falls 15% to 8,500. Revenue drops 6.5%, but profit rises from $200,000 to $212,500. Raise it, and watch customer reaction.

Market entry

A meal kit company eyes a city of 800,000 households. They expect 5% to sign up and order twice a month at $30, with a 25% contribution margin. Local fixed costs are $450,000 a month. Enter? Contribution is 40,000 households × 2 × $30 × 25% = $600,000 a month, so profit is $150,000. Break even adoption is 3.75%. Enter, but test whether 5% is realistic first.

Why did profit fall?

Split it into revenue and costs, then split each again. If revenue rose 5% but costs rose 9%, find which cost line moved most. Labor, rent and materials are the usual suspects.

How to practice

Do two or three timed cases a day and say your structure out loud. See how to practice case interviews for free for more.

Questions

What questions come up in business operations interviews?

Break even, capacity and staffing, pricing with elasticity, profitability drivers and market sizing.

How should I structure an ops case?

Clarify the goal, break it into drivers, calculate out loud, then make a decision and name the main risk.

Do I need finance knowledge for strategy roles?

The basics help: margins, break even, NPV and cash flow. Most cases need clear arithmetic more than finance theory.
