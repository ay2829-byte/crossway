How to find jobs that sponsor visas in 2026

A practical guide for international students and workers: which employers sponsor, how to check before you apply, and the visa types worth knowing about.

If you need visa sponsorship, the job hunt has an extra filter most people never think about. The good news: sponsorship is more common than it feels, and you can check most employers before you spend time applying.

This is a practical guide, not legal advice. Immigration rules change often, so confirm details with the employer's immigration team or an immigration attorney.

Know your timeline first

  • F-1 OPT gives most graduates 12 months of work authorization after their degree.
  • STEM OPT can extend that by 24 months if your degree is on the STEM list and your employer uses E-Verify.
  • H-1B is the most common long term work visa. Most employers go through an annual lottery with a cap of 65,000 visas plus 20,000 for people with a US master's degree or higher.

Knowing how much OPT time you have tells you how urgent sponsorship is and which employers make sense.

Employers who skip the lottery

Some employers are cap exempt, which means their H-1B petitions don't go through the lottery and can be filed any time of year. These include universities, nonprofits affiliated with universities, and nonprofit or government research organizations. If timing is tight, these are worth a serious look.

Check before you apply

  • USCIS H-1B Employer Data Hub. Public data on which companies filed H-1B petitions and how many were approved.
  • Department of Labor disclosure data. Labor condition applications list employers, job titles and salaries for H-1B roles.
  • The posting itself. Many postings say "sponsorship available" or "unable to sponsor". Search the text before you apply.
  • Ask early. It's fine to ask the recruiter in the first call. It saves everyone time.

Other visas worth knowing

  • O-1 for people with extraordinary ability, shown through awards, publications or press.
  • TN for Canadian and Mexican citizens in listed professions.
  • E-3 for Australian citizens, with its own annual allocation.
  • L-1 for transfers within the same company after working for it abroad.

Make sponsoring you an easy yes

Sponsorship costs a company time and money, so give them a clear reason. Proof beats claims here. A record of real work problems solved in your field, a few case studies, and strong references make it easier for a hiring manager to argue for you.

Questions

Which companies sponsor H-1B visas?

Large tech, consulting and finance firms file the most petitions, but thousands of smaller companies sponsor too. The USCIS H-1B Employer Data Hub lists them.

What does cap exempt mean?

Cap exempt employers, such as universities and affiliated nonprofits, can file H-1B petitions outside the annual lottery.

Should I mention sponsorship in my application?

Be honest if asked, and ask the recruiter early. Setting it once on your Crossway profile means companies already know before they invite you.
