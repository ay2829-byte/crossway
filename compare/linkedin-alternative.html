Why the application pile is broken

Most job seekers send hundreds of applications. Recruiters skim each resume for seconds, and software filters out good people for missing a keyword. The people who get through are often the best at writing resumes, not the best at the work.

Crossway flips it. Companies post a role and see their 10 best matches right away, ranked with clear reasons. Members show what they can do with daily drills, posts about real work and Arena problems that companies can open and read.

Questions

Is Crossway a replacement for LinkedIn?

Not for networking or news. Crossway replaces the part of LinkedIn that is broken for job seekers: applying into a pile and hoping. On Crossway you don't apply. Companies see their best matches and invite them.

Do I need a resume?

No. You build one profile with your skills, experience, projects and proof of work. Companies match on skills, not keywords in a PDF.

Can companies see my name?

Not at first. Companies see a candidate code, your skills and your work. Your name, photo and school unlock only when you accept their invite.

What does it cost?

Crossway is free for members. Companies can post a role and see their top matches for free.

How do companies decide who to invite?

They see a ranked list of matches with the reasons behind each one: skills, experience, pay fit and proof of work like Arena solves, posts and nods from other people.
