Privacy Policy

Last updated October 25, 2026

Crossway is a free career network. You prove your skills in Arena, get matched to jobs, and get invited by companies. Crossway also includes Crossway Projects, a free project tracker. This page explains what we collect, who can see it and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email, and your name and photo if you sign in with Google. We never see your Google password.
  • Your profile: your name, headline, field, years of experience, skills, the places you want to work, your birthdate, and any projects, links or files you add.
  • LinkedIn PDF import: if you import your LinkedIn profile PDF, it is read in your browser to fill in your profile. The file itself is not uploaded or stored. Only the fields you save are kept.
  • Arena activity: your answers, scores and solve times. For timed problems we record how you wrote your solution (keystrokes, pastes and leaving the page) so it can be replayed. Companies can view the replay only for problems you solved and only once you're matched with them.
  • Community activity: your posts, drill answers, nods, follows and streaks.
  • Job activity: the jobs you open, save, hide and apply to, so we can improve your matches.
  • Crossway Projects: the projects, tasks, notes and dates you create.
  • Basic usage: when you signed up and last signed in, and overall numbers like how many people use each feature.

Who can see what

  • Other members can see your public profile: your name, headline, field, skills, posts, proof of work and Arena results.
  • Companies see matched candidates blind. They see your skills, experience level and what you've proven, but not your name, photo or school until you accept their invite.
  • Only you can see your birthdate, your email, your job activity and your Crossway Projects data.

How we use it

To run Crossway: sign you in, build your profile, rank your job matches, show your work to the right companies, and keep your streaks and leagues. We do not sell your information, share it with advertisers, or let anyone pay to see more of it.

Jobs from other sites

Job listings come from public company career pages. When you click Apply you go to that company's own site, and what you share there is covered by their privacy policy, not ours.

Where it is stored

Your data is stored with Supabase, our database and sign in provider, and the site is hosted on Vercel. Access to your data is limited by row level security, so each account can only read what it is allowed to.

Your choices

You can edit or remove anything on your profile at any time. To delete your whole account and everything in it, email us and we will remove it within 30 days.
