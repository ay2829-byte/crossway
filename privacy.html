Privacy Policy

Last updated September 28, 2026

Crossway is a free project and task tracker. This page explains what information we keep and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, and your name if you give it or sign in with Google.
  • Your content: the projects, tasks, notes, checklists and dates you create.
  • Basic activity: when you created your account and when you last signed in.

How we use it

Only to run Crossway: to sign you in, save your work, and show it back to you. We also look at overall numbers, such as how many people signed up, to understand how the app is used.

Who can see your content

Your projects and tasks are private to your account. Other users cannot see them. We do not sell your information or share it with advertisers.

Where it is stored

Your data is stored with Supabase, our database and sign in provider, and the website is hosted on Vercel. If you sign in with Google, Google shares your name, email and profile picture with us. We never see your Google password.

Deleting your data

You can delete any project or task at any time. To delete your whole account and everything in it, email us and we will remove it.
